# Unit-invariant paired calcium follow-up

This separate protocol is frozen after the absolute-calcium support failure but before any calcium/pH exposure contrast. The original result remains unchanged: 7 qualified eICU / 526 MIMIC people. A metadata/quality attrition check found 98 eICU people (36 exposed) with qualified pH at both fixed calcium timestamps and some matching calcium unit tuple across the two times. That is an optimistic ceiling; latest-entry, unit-tuple uniqueness and native calcium validity still require checking. No effect estimate motivated this continuation.

The biological question is unchanged: original SpO2 instability precedes a calcium decrease accompanied by alkalinization. Use exactly the original selected people, assay IDs, baseline and follow-up timestamps, hospital linkage and qualified pH measurements from `SPO2_IONIZED_CALCIUM_PLAN.md`. No new patient source, replacement sample, pH window or mortality endpoint is introduced.

The newly chosen calcium estimand is the exposed-minus-unexposed mean log(follow-up / baseline) of native recorded ionized calcium. Exponentiation gives a ratio of geometric fold changes. If both measurements share the same multiplicative conversion, that conversion cancels exactly. This does not determine absolute calcium or prove analyzer calibration is stable. Stable unit metadata support the assumption but do not exclude undocumented changes in reporting or testing.

For calcium retain the original all-entry-times-present, latest-entry, finite value, explicit censor and tied-value rules. Also require a single normalized unit tuple among all entries at each selected timestamp, identical tuples between baseline and follow-up. In eICU allow system `mg/dl` with interface `mg/dl`, `mmol/l` or `meq/l`; in MIMIC require system `mmol/l` and empty interface. Do not decide which conflicting unit field is correct. A unit tuple supplies a set of possible units, not an inferred conversion. Require at least one **common** candidate unit under which both native values lie in the original broad [0.1,3.0] mmol/L range; candidate factors are 4.0078 for mg/dL, 1 for mmol/L and 2 for mEq/L. Do not use differing candidate units for the two times. Native ratios, not converted concentrations, enter the analysis.

For MIMIC retain the original complete unique specimen-ID rule and equality with the pH specimen at each timestamp. eICU remains an exact-time approximation without specimen identifiers. Preserve the same joint support limits: 50 people / 20 in each exposure arm per source, at least ten eICU hospitals, no missing eICU hospital ID. No count-dependent further loosening.

The four primary contrasts are log calcium-fold-change and pH change in each database, with all four reported. Use 20,000 whole-hospital bootstrap draws in eICU (seed 2026090567) and whole-person draws in MIMIC (seed 2026090568), identical resamples for the two analytes. Report 95% and Bonferroni 98.75% percentile intervals. Any missing-arm resample invalidates advancement rather than being discarded. Advance only if both simultaneous calcium log-contrast upper bounds are below zero and both pH lower bounds above zero. This is an unadjusted biochemical screen; it is not novel biology, a causal mechanism, a treatment effect or mortality benefit by itself.

Independent SQL/Python verification must reproduce timestamp unit tuples, quality flags, possible-unit sets, matched fixed calcium/pH samples and final person sets, log ratios and exposure contrasts. Explicitly verify algebraic cancellation for every shared candidate conversion without adopting that conversion as truth. Directly expand 100 bootstrap draws/source. Retain all person records privately and aggregate results in the primary notebook. Label actual execution local or Colab. Baseline severity, testing, sample timing, regression to the mean, transfusions/citrate, treatment, renal replacement and unobserved analyzer changes remain alternative explanations. No further threshold, unit, window or outcome search is triggered by a failed result.
