# Heart-rate change during the first SpO2 instability transition

Specified before this joint phenotype or its mortality counts are calculated. Scope is the original 11,452 eICU and 4,711 MIMIC dynamics-eligible HF encounters, using existing audited first-four-hour vital bins. All previous negative experiments remain unchanged. Both databases have already been explored; this is a newly specified contrast, not an untouched prospective validation sample.

## Question and limits

Does a fall in charted SpO2 with a contemporaneous heart-rate fall identify substantially higher subsequent hospital mortality than a fall with a heart-rate rise? Is that difference larger than the corresponding difference during rising SpO2? The second comparison is intended to challenge a nonspecific association with heart-rate decline. It is not a biologically inert negative control.

The broad hypoxic HR response is already known, including [HF physiology](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39962768/) and [sleep-apnea cardiovascular outcome work](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8483223/). Our charted 15-minute states cannot measure a breath-level chemoreflex, autonomic reserve, cardiac output or proven arterial hypoxia. The earlier two-sided arterial-gas audit correctly found no adequate paired-sample support; that does not establish either artifact or arterial agreement. This study tests a potentially important clinical physiological pattern, without claiming that the broad mechanism is new.

## Phenotype, fixed before outcomes

Retain the preceding and current bin numbers, values and representative times. Rising versus falling SpO2 is the sign of this same first transition. Do not find a later drop or replace an unavailable measurement.

Choose the smallest exposed stay_id per person before HR availability or values are joined. Join the existing HR median in each of those same 15-minute bins. Require both HR representative times within five minutes of their respective SpO2 representative times. A fall is HR change ≤−5 beats/min, a rise ≥+5, and intermediate values form a separately reported flat category. Missing or unmatched HR remains unavailable. The five-beat threshold is investigator-selected to avoid classifying a one-beat rounding change as a substantial direction; it is not an established physiological threshold. No threshold sensitivity or optimization is planned.

Independently reproduce first transition, person selection, HR timing and all direction labels in Python and SQL. Record all support counts before joining hospital mortality. Keep patient records private in Drive Data and all protocol/results in the single primary notebook.

## Mortality screen

Use original hospital_mortality after the four-hour landmark; retain unknown outcomes separately. Confirm the original landmark exclusions and person identity. Report all six observed direction groups (SpO2 up/down × HR fall/flat/rise), but the primary contrast uses only four groups with nonflat HR. For each source require at least 25 known outcomes and at least five deaths and five survivors in each of those four groups; eICU also requires at least 20 represented hospitals and complete hospital identifiers in its primary sample. These are minimal stability checks, not a power guarantee. If either database fails, report its observed counts and stop before calculating comparative effects or bootstrap intervals in either source.

Primary estimands in each database are (1) mortality risk difference between HR-fall and HR-rise patients during falling SpO2, and (2) that risk difference minus the HR-fall versus HR-rise difference during rising SpO2. These are crude associations, not treatment effects. No outcome regression or causal attribution is part of this initial large-effect screen.

Use 20,000 nonparametric bootstrap draws: resample whole eICU hospitals, preserving all selected people in each sampled hospital; resample MIMIC people. Fixed NumPy PCG64 seeds 2026090541 and 2026090542 respectively. For MIMIC, multinomial resampling of the eight direction/outcome cell counts is exactly the corresponding patient bootstrap for these statistics. For eICU, multinomial hospital weights multiply the observed hospital-specific eight-cell counts. Validate bootstrap arithmetic with an independent direct expansion of 100 fixed resamples. Report percentile 95% intervals and Bonferroni 98.75% intervals for the four primary estimands. If fewer than 99% of bootstrap draws have all four nonempty group denominators, do not grade the interval. No point-mass clipping or missing-as-surviving imputation.

Advance only if, in BOTH sources, the multiplicity-adjusted lower bound for the falling-SpO2 risk difference exceeds five percentage points and the lower bound for the direction interaction exceeds zero. This is a research prioritization criterion, not a clinical cutoff. If it fails, close this specification without subgroup or threshold searches. If it passes, the result is still provisional: measured severity, absolute HR/SpO2, beta-blockers, sedatives, pacing/arrhythmias, ventilation, vasopressors, observation patterns and informative treatment/testing require a separately frozen confounding analysis and independent clinical validation before any biological or mortality-benefit claim. Mortality prediction alone does not establish the requested discovery.
