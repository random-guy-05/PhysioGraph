# PhysioGraph biological candidate triage

Scope: MIMIC and eICU patient data only. Literature is used to assess novelty,
not as an additional experimental dataset. No candidate below establishes a
paradigm-shifting discovery or a mortality benefit.

| Candidate | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| Event-aligned capnography to distinguish ventilatory from perfusion-compatible disturbance | Full eICU monitor and MIMIC chart metadata scans completed locally; interrupted hashing resumed from committed tables, with both full native hashes matching prior extractions. Five-minute paired timing support: 43 eICU people (23 SpO2 up/20 down; 25 hospitals) and five MIMIC (two up/three down). Both fixed support floors fail. Context timestamps on both sides: respiratory-rate presence 40 eICU, minute volume one MIMIC; neither verifies constant ventilation. Independent original episodes, nearest times, forward order and context flags agree. No CO2 amplitude, mortality or treatment model; the mechanism remains unresolved. Generic EtCO2 prognosis and SpO2–CO2 oscillations in HF are already published. See SPO2_CAPNOGRAPHY_FEASIBILITY_RESULTS.md. |
| Acute coupled potassium fall and glucose rise after an SpO2 episode | Completed frozen native-chemistry experiment with fixed episode/sham anchors in 152 eICU people/61 hospitals and 134 MIMIC people. Joint events: 0/56 exposed vs 4/96 controls, and 1/37 vs 3/97. Risk differences −4.167 and −0.390 pp; both event-count gates fail, so no bootstrap inference or adjusted/mortality model. Exact-decimal, unit, specimen, anchor, source-hash and independent joint-label checks passed. No threshold or endpoint rescue. Human epinephrine experiments motivate the biology, but the coupled event is not a catecholamine assay; generic glucose/potassium-ratio prognosis in HF already exists. See SPO2_ADRENERGIC_METABOLIC_RESULTS.md. |
| Calcium decline with alkalinization after early SpO2 instability | Original absolute-unit joint support failed: 7 eICU / 526 MIMIC. A separately locked native-ratio study retained 98 eICU people in 17 hospitals and 526 MIMIC people using identical unit tuples and common possible conversion sets across fixed samples. Calcium geometric fold-change ratios: 0.990985 (98.75% CI 0.930496–1.014992), 0.999429 (0.962104–1.038802); pH contrasts +0.008840 and +0.013343 both cross zero. All four conventional 95% intervals also cross null. No directional replication. Independent native-quality, pairing, common-unit cancellation and bootstrap checks passed. Ratios do not establish absolute calcium units, analyzer stability, binding, depletion, intracellular calcium or contractile failure. See SPO2_CALCIUM_RELATIVE_RESULTS.md. |
| Hemoglobin oxidation after early SpO2 instability | Full raw lab scans completed. Fixed MetHb timing pairs: 346 eICU, zero MIMIC; original joint gate failed before numeric qualification. A separate protocol locked the hospital split before values: qualified eICU samples 85/237 people, 19/30 disjoint hospitals. Change contrasts -0.054102 pp (97.5% CI -0.248151 to 0.153531) and -0.008073 pp (-0.099911 to 0.058345); both fail positive advancement. Independent quality, pairing, split and hospital-bootstrap validation passed. Modified cell-free Hb reoxygenation experiments are indirect evidence, not adult HF proof; no oxidation flux, mortality or treatment claim. See SPO2_OXIDATION_HOSPITAL_RESULTS.md. |
| Persistent HR elevation after saturation returns near baseline | Completed the frozen recovery/sham mortality screen in 3,613 eICU and 322 MIMIC people. Within-desaturation HR odds ratios per 10 bpm: 1.1556 (95% CI 0.9276–1.4396) and 1.3052 (0.8357–2.0385); specificity interactions 1.0254 and 1.3110, both intervals crossing one. All four Holm p>0.78. Both support floors and independent phenotype/covariance checks passed. No positive advancement; generic HR-recovery prognosis is already established. See SPO2_RECOVERY_HYSTERESIS_RESULTS.md. |
| Direct cardiac-output decline after original SpO2 instability | Full raw MIMIC metadata scan completed. CCO pairs: 73/95 people in fixed internal splits, only 9/5 exposed; thermodilution pairs: 73/75, only 6/12 exposed. No modality meets the fixed support floor. No CO values or mortality read, and no eICU flow surrogate substituted. This limits this specification, not all circulatory biology. See SPO2_DIRECT_FLOW_RESULTS.md. |
| Raw laboratory recovery beyond the arterial cache | Full raw scans recovered 8 eICU/40 MIMIC common-window rows; no additional two-sided original-transition pairs. Permissive timing ceilings remain 0/2 people at ±5 minutes and 2/9 at ±15. All legacy rows and independent timing/hospital-linkage checks validate. Extra fluid-pO2 labels are not assumed arterial. See SPO2_ARTERIAL_RAW_RECOVERY_RESULTS.md. |
| Within-patient oxygen–troponin coupling, with independent hospital groups in eICU | Completed an explicitly separate amendment after the original MIMIC support failure, with the hash-based split fixed before outcome changes. Discovery: 608 people/68 hospitals, ratio of troponin fold changes 0.9503 (95% CI 0.8916–1.0129), Holm p=0.2313. Validation: 460/70, ratio 0.9627 (0.8557–1.0830), Holm p=0.5217. No positive internal validation. The pre-estimate lag-omission diagnostic also fails (0.9591 and 1.0225; both secondary Holm p=0.9726). MAP missingness 84.7%/90.2%, short-panel bias, treatment and observation confounding remain. No mortality model or new mechanism. See SPO2_EICU_HOSPITAL_RESULTS.md. |
| Within-patient consecutive troponin changes through the existing 28-hour window, MIMIC/eICU | New raw vital extraction and independent validation completed. Laboratory timing supported 1,090 eICU/293 MIMIC people; repeated usable oxygen windows supported 1,068/16, with 419/five instability switchers. The frozen joint gate fails; no troponin changes or models were computed under this specification. The MIMIC data cannot support this temporal definition. Subsequent eICU work is an explicit separate amendment, not a changed primary gate or cross-database replication. See SPO2_WITHIN_PERSON_TROPONIN_RESULTS.md. |
| Alkalosis/acetazolamide as a purported new explanation or treatment of HF oxygen instability | Literature triage only; no new patient model. A [12-patient double-blind crossover HF study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16239622/) already reduced central apnea and time below 90% saturation with acetazolamide. A [six-patient mechanistic crossover study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24251826/) found improved apnea despite an increased hypercapnic ventilatory response. These small studies do not establish mortality benefit, but they rule out a first-discovery claim for the broad therapeutic idea and caution against a simplistic chemosensitivity explanation. |
| First-specimen troponin emergence after an explicit baseline upper bound | Completed frozen local interval analysis with original SpO2 exposure. At 12 hours, definite events: eICU 6/147 exposed versus 7/190 unexposed; MIMIC 2/18 versus 9/44. Descriptive RDs +0.40 pp (95% CI -3.93 to +5.31) and -9.34 pp (-25.51 to +14.26). Both adjusted models fail fixed support gates; 24-hour sensitivities also cross zero. No positive replication, no mortality analysis. Unmeasured/indeterminate tests are not negative, and U is not an exact baseline or an assumed 99th percentile. Troponin conversion itself already appears in [PROTECT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21900185/) and a [serial AHF study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22407461/); a new ICU threshold would not establish a new mechanism. See SPO2_TROPONIN_EMERGENCE_RESULTS.md. |
| Recovery of omitted troponin reporting bounds and selection into the measured cohort | Completed original-ID MIMIC comment extraction and independent selection audit. The strict whole-comment parser found no standalone values. A separately documented whitelist amendment then recovered 598 `<0.01` and 3 `>25` MIMIC records, all lacking numeric values, while treating the accompanying 0.10 ng/mL interpretation statement as metadata, never a patient result or MI diagnosis. In eICU, discarded bounds create an optimistic ceiling of 431 additional 12-hour timed pairs; 14 existing pairs have an omitted follow-up bound and none a later omitted baseline bound. The corresponding 24-hour counts are 337 and 19. These are measurement/selection findings, not rise endpoints or evidence of a mechanism. See SPO2_TROPONIN_REPORTING_RECOVERY_RESULTS.md and SPO2_TROPONIN_COMMENT_TEMPLATE_RESULTS.md. |
| Numeric troponin rises reflect misread reporting limits | Completed the frozen original-file audit: 13,682 eICU and 4,758 MIMIC source rows. Retained eICU numeric results have matching exact text; 1,800 explicitly bounded records are outside that numeric pipeline. All 4,137 retained MIMIC numeric records have the literal `___` as text, so the original reporting qualifiers cannot be checked from that field. Current source values, medians, availability, ratios and labels reconstruct exactly; SQL/Python flag validation passes. This neither proves false rises nor resolves selection from omitted bounded results. See SPO2_TROPONIN_ASSAY_AUDIT_RESULTS.md. |
| Plasma-volume correction of troponin using serial Hb/Hct | Literature triage rejects treating this as measured biomarker mass or a new mechanism. [Direct volume validation in HF](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30098381/) found poor correspondence between estimated and measured volume changes; [acute-HF validation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8788058/) likewise questions individual change estimates. No patient model or corrected troponin endpoint was run. Serial concentration biology remains possible, but the formula cannot establish it. See SPO2_MECHANISM_REVIEW.md. |
| Troponin association driven by taking a maximum over more follow-up draws | Completed a new frozen paired sensitivity in the current four-hour-landmark raw-replay cohort: 2,294 eICU / 774 MIMIC encounters at 12 hours. Replacing the maximum with the exact expectation from one uniformly selected observed timestamp changes the adjusted exposure association by +0.75 pp in eICU (maximum minus one-sample, 95% CI -0.10 to +1.60) and -1.12 pp in MIMIC (-2.48 to +0.24), both Holm p=0.1649. The 24-hour contrasts also cross zero. No consistent support for this explanation; this is not equivalence or proof against selective testing. Native labels reconstruct exactly; the prospectively specified decimal labels differ from legacy floating-point labels in 11 overlapping horizon-specific rows. Independent joins, outcome aggregation, coefficients and covariance validate. No mortality or novel mechanism established. See SPO2_TROPONIN_OPPORTUNITY_RESULTS.md. |
| Independent arterial oxygen changes across the original SpO2 transition | Completed the frozen timing-only audit of existing arterial-gas components. At +/-5 minutes, neither database has a qualified two-sided PaO2 pair; complete-window denominators are 4,302 eICU and 1,371 MIMIC episodes. At +/-15 minutes only 2 eICU / 4 MIMIC people qualify. Even ignoring source, unit, value and revision quality yields only 0/2 raw timing pairs at +/-5 minutes and 2/9 at +/-15. These ceilings apply only to the existing extraction. SQL/Python patient sets and source hashes agree. The cached-pairing specification is closed without reading oxygen changes or mortality; it cannot distinguish physiology from artifact. Binned SpO2 medians and representative times are not exact simultaneous measurements, and PaO2 is not measured SaO2. See SPO2_ARTERIAL_PAIRING_RESULTS.md. |
| Serial-troponin descriptive amendment after the failed primary gate | Explicitly departed from the earlier stop rule after counts were known, before directions were examined. All 133 eICU / 76 MIMIC complete trajectories retained the original time/assay rules. Pre-episode rises: 44/133 and 32/76; post-only rises: 15/133 and 11/76. Full four-pattern tables and the unchanged one-person sensitivity are reported, without hypothesis tests or mortality associations. SQL/Python concentrations and labels agree. This concerns first captured ICU episodes and recorded numeric values, not injury onset or the first possible hypoxic event. It does not rescue the primary gate or establish a novel mechanism. See SPO2_TROPONIN_DESCRIPTIVE_RESULTS.md. |
| CVP changes preceding the first original SpO2 episode | Completed local frozen extraction and independent validation. Complete timing: 66 encounters / 65 people in eICU, 6 / 6 in MIMIC. | Low diastolic pressure combined with hypoxia as a new myocardial-injury mechanism | Literature triage only; no new patient model. A [J-HOP study of 840 cardiovascular-risk patients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8029661/) already associated low nighttime DBP and nocturnal hypoxia with elevated hs-troponin, including their combined phenotype. It is not an ICU HF replication or causal test, but the broad mechanism is not novel. Changing the population or cutoff alone would not meet the goal. |
| A combined hypotension/hypoxemia mortality score | Literature triage only; no new patient model. An [August 2026 eICU cardiovascular cohort study](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/16/8/e123424) already combined first-day hypotension, hypoxemia, tachycardia and ventilation, and examined a hypoxemia–hypotension interaction. Its additive interaction was exploratory, without multiple-testing correction or causal interpretation. Its 24-hour summaries differ from our four-hour episode timing. A renamed score or generic joint-risk association is not a new biological discovery. |
| Preferential cardiac biomarker association versus renal deterioration | Completed the locked paired troponin/creatinine contrast in the same patients and windows, with a documented post-estimate decimal-threshold correction and archived preliminary results. | Low saturation combined with low blood pressure | Already associated with prognosis in a [2009 acute-HF cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19372679/). Merely recovering this association would not be a new biological finding. |
| Heart-rate response to transient desaturation | The breath-level mechanism is not resolved by the current 15-minute bins. [Prior work](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8483223/) linked event-related pulse-rate responses to cardiovascular outcomes in sleep apnea, and a [2025 HF physiological study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39962768/) studied transient hypoxia. A separate coarse first-transition HR-direction mortality screen has now completed: HR-fall versus HR-rise risk differences during falling SpO2 were +1.25 pp in eICU and +9.87 pp in MIMIC; all primary uncertainty intervals cross zero and the rising-SpO2 comparison also fails. No replicated large-risk pattern or autonomic mechanism is established. See SPO2_HEART_RATE_POLARITY_RESULTS.md. |

The heart-rate literature does not answer every possible ICU question, but
it rejects a first-discovery claim for the broad mechanism. A new score in a
different setting would require a substantive biological or clinical advance,
not just a new name or a significant regression coefficient.

September 5, 2026 novelty refresh: the [REDOX-AHF protocol](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42172256/)
already randomizes restrictive versus liberal oxygen targets in acute HF,
with pulmonary congestion as its primary outcome. Routine oxygen targeting
cannot be presented as this project's new biological idea. A separate
[2026 MIMIC-IV HF/AF study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42026493/)
already examines charted HR variability and early changes against mortality.
This also precludes a first-discovery claim for generic HR variability in HF;
it does not by itself answer the distinct oxygen–HR coupling question.

For the hemoglobin candidate, [human oximeter experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2352007/)
also found anemia-related measurement error during profound hypoxemia.
An interaction between hemoglobin and charted SpO2 therefore has both biological
and instrument-related explanations. The frozen feasibility extraction is
unchanged; the completed interaction experiment does not establish a mechanism
or exclude this instrument-related alternative. This is an interpretation limit, not a replacement
for the biological objective.

Keep unsupported hypotheses and implementation corrections visible. No
effect estimate was fitted in the provisional circulatory-context run:
the event or prespecified model-complexity gates prevented all four primary
models. Final reporting must use the corrected extraction and distinguish
insufficient evidence from evidence that the biological effect is absent.

The outcome-blind MIMIC laboratory linkage audit completed and passed its
known-ID validation criterion for CBC and troponin, identifying 2,681 additional
CBC and 2,009 troponin records for eligible encounters. A documented linkage-only
amendment completed the unchanged biological feasibility algorithms on
augmented records. Hb now passes; injury ordering still fails with 76 MIMIC
trajectories. The original explicit-ID-only failures remain unchanged.
See MIMIC_PREICU_LAB_LINKAGE_RESULTS.md and PREICU_LAB_LINKAGE_AMENDMENT.md.
A linkage result is not a biological discovery.

The [1996 Mortara physiological study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8813833/)
already connected HF saturation instability to periodic breathing and tested
controlled breathing. The present charted-jump phenotype is not automatically
the same physiology, and that broad mechanism is not novel. SERVE-HF and
ADVENT-HF further caution against inferring survival benefit from correction
of respiratory surrogates; see SPO2_MECHANISM_REVIEW.md for the distinct trials
and their primary-source results.

September 5 diagnostic-ECG resource refresh: the [open MIMIC-IV diagnostic
ECG release](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-ecg/1.0/) is a distinct
potential source from the small waveform pilot and the local ST checkbox.
Completed original-cohort linkage finds 2,921 encounters with a nominal
in-hospital ECG and 341 with nominal pre-ICU/later pairs. The source warns
of unsynchronized ECG machine clocks. The separately frozen charted-EKG
comparison finds 711 isolated candidate pairs, 460 within 15 minutes and
618 within an hour, with substantial tails. These are not validated acquisition
links; no timestamps were corrected. See SPO2_ECG_LINKAGE_RESULTS.md and
SPO2_ECG_CLOCK_RESULTS.md. Metadata availability is not biological evidence.

Generic electrical consequences of hypoxia are also not novel: a
[1991 HF study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1907836/) reported concurrent
desaturation and arrhythmias/ST depression, and a
[2024 sleep-apnea study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11380986/)
examined event-related repolarization lability. A diagnostic ECG association
with charted instability would need a distinct, defensible biological advance
and validation; these prior findings cannot be relabeled as our discovery.

The subsequent frozen paired-ECG repolarization-versus-conduction experiment
completed in 124 discovery/85 validation people from the original MIMIC
cohort. JTcF change contrasts were +0.011 ms (95% CI −12.759 to +12.782)
and −0.680 ms (−17.074 to +15.714); specificity contrasts also cross zero,
all four primary Holm p=1.0. Close this specification to positive promotion.
Do not try alternate rate corrections to rescue it. The timing-margin and
same-device sensitivities lack support, and pre-result rate-correction and
rhythm-selection critiques remain. See SPO2_ECG_REPOLARIZATION_RESULTS.md.
The [2018 MESA study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29963729/) also establishes
prior QRS–T-angle associations with sleep-disordered breathing; that generic
mechanism is not a novel alternative endpoint to claim here.
