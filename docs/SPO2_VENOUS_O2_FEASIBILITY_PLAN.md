# SpO2–venous oxygen reserve: outcome-blind feasibility specification

Frozen before the new raw-source support scan on 2026-09-06. This document
authorizes **Phase A only**. It does not authorize physiology effect estimates,
troponin analysis, mortality analysis, threshold changes, or the predefined
fallback experiment.

## Cohort and anchors

Reuse the production adult explicit-heart-failure cohort, first ICU stay per
hospital encounter, early ICU/cardiogenic-shock qualification, four-hour
survival/observation boundary, and strict SpO2 dynamics eligibility. Reuse the
established SpO2 preprocessing: values 50–100%, same-time medians, 15-minute
median bins, at least three bins, at least two forward transitions no more than
30 minutes apart, and the first absolute transition of at least four percentage
points. No-episode controls use the existing deterministic transition nearest
the corresponding quantile of the exposed anchor-time distribution.

## Original-source audit

Scan the credentialed CSV sources under `My Drive/Data/eICU/Full` and
`My Drive/Data/MIMIC/Full`. Do not infer venous-oxygen availability from an old
feature or endpoint table. Preserve dataset, person/stay, event time, value,
unit, raw source/item and compartment privately. Aggregate repository outputs
must distinguish `mixed_venous`, `central_venous`, and `venous_unspecified`.
A generic oxygen-saturation label is not venous. Unknown or conflicting units
are not accepted as percentage saturation.

Audit hours 0–4 for oxygenation, pressure/heart-rate/CVP/pulmonary-artery
signals, cardiac output/index/power, hemoglobin/hematocrit, lactate,
creatinine, respiratory support/FiO2, and baseline vasoactive support.
Venous-oxygen rows may be scanned through hour 6 solely to evaluate the frozen
pre/post windows around anchors:

- PRE: `[-60, 0)` minutes relative to anchor.
- POST: `(+30, +120]` minutes relative to anchor.

No change in venous oxygen and no clinical endpoint is calculated in Phase A.

## Feasibility gates

For each database and compartment, primary support requires at least 300 valid
pre/post encounters, including at least 100 exposed and 100 controls.
Exploratory support requires at least 200 total and at least 75 per arm.
Measurement choice is determined only by provenance and these counts:

1. supported mixed venous saturation in both databases;
2. otherwise supported central venous saturation in both;
3. otherwise source-specific supported compartments, explicitly without a
   same-measure replication claim;
4. otherwise stop and recommend the predefined oxygen-delivery proxy fallback.

The decision is generated by code. A failed venous-oxygen gate cannot be
rescued by inspecting troponin, mortality, lactate trajectories, pressors, or
another downstream outcome.
