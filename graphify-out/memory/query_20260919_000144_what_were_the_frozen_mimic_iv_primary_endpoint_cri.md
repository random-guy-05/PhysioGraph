# Q: What were the frozen MIMIC-IV primary endpoint criteria?

## Answer

First qualifying event during ICU minutes greater than 360 through 960 when a pressure or support component and a hypoperfusion or organ-injury component occurred within 360 minutes; endpoint time was the later component. Pressure/support was two consecutive hourly cuff bins with SBP below 90 or MAP below 65, positive continuous vasoactive or inotropic infusion, or new IABP Impella ECMO. Hypoperfusion was lactate, oliguria, creatinine, ALT, or pH meeting frozen thresholds.
